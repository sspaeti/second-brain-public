Categories

Every note has one category assigned, so if you are new here, you can explore by category. With 700+ notes and 1000+ interlinks, it can be hard to know where to start. With categories, next to Map of Content, you can choose a starting point and go from there.

Another option is also to browse through the topic cards on the Second Brain or Data Engineering Vault.
